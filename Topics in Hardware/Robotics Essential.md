# Motor
- *No load:* no resistance, free spin, largest rpm
- *Max efficiency:* optimal torque & speed
- *Stall:* max torque, not moving, max current
- *PWM:* 3.3-5V; 20ms (50Hz), longer pulse from the **threshold** means faster - vice versa
	- Threshold to determine the direction of rotation
	- Saturation: max speed, doesn't matter if the distance from threshold increase 
# IMU
- *Magnetic:* measure direction like compass
- *Acceleration:* high-speed application
- *Gyro:* rotation measurement
