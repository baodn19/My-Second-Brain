# MSRP
## Overview
- *Mission:* 
	- Support students with **diverse life experiences**, academic interests, and background
	- Promote grad education as **meaningful & attainable goal**
	- **Broaden perspectives** & participation in research and innovation
- *Criteria:*
	- Aligns with disciplines where research limited due to **structural, institutional, or resource-related barriers**
	- strong academic potential by overcoming **economic, social, educational barriers**: financial aid, first gen college, limited access academic resource
	- Life experiences and background reflect deep understand of **structural and systemic challenges** impact access to 
	- Leadership advancing **belonging, achievement, and composition**
- *Time:* June 8 - August 8, 2026
- *Benefit:*
	- *Stipend:* $5400
	- Grad application fee waiver
- *Pillars:*
	- *Research:* 40hours/ week to research activity; answer interesting research question
	- *Co-curricular instruction:* communicate research & navigate grad school

## Evaluation
- *Phase:*
	1. Remove incomplete and ineligible
	2. Each application is reviewed by 3 ARC members
	3. Faculty have final decision who to work with: select clear understanding of group; clear alignment of how I can contribute
- *Criteria:*
	- 30% *Research Potential*: concrete research experience, contribution, learn, skills; future plan for grad school
	- 20% *Academic Potential*: quantitative metrics; what course, how perform; LOR speak to this
	- 20% *Leadership & Personal Attributes*: leadership roles; **initiative** to improve community; how work through challenges
	- 20% *LOR*
	- 10% *Application Quality*: attention to details with different aspects
- *Faculty Mentor:* describe and **why you want to work with them**
	1. mit.edu/msrp go live next week; pick from the list; added faculty mentor daily; list faculties that aren't on the list
	2. 3 faculty mentors
	3. Specify interest in research topic
	- Save the faculty preference for last when ready to submit
	- Do not contact faculty
- *LOR:* provide information packet (the program; application material; answer to short questions)
- *Tips for strong response:*
	- Be **authentic**
	- Talk **concretely** about academic goals
	- Show **alignment** with MSRP (what the ethos is)
	- Demonstrate **growth & initiative** 
	- Be **concise**, get to the point
	- Use **STAR** method: when did you implement **creativity**
	- **Keywords** show alignment with faculty
- *Talking about research:*
	- Clear motivation for exploring: take advantages in with low-resources
	- Initiative & readiness
	- Show knowledge of research at MIT
	- Skills section to highlight transferable strengths
- *Do:*
	- Research MIT & academic offerings
	- Tell your story
	- Highlight potential: **transferable skills**
	- Tailor application
	- Polish materials
	- Explore different research areas
	- Contact graduate students for learning more about research **independently of MSRP**
	- LOR must be tailored **1000%** to MSRP
- *Don't:*
	- Contact MIT faculty
	- Generic
	- Last minute
	- Interested in 1 prof
	- CV/ Resume in essay form; go more than 1.5 pages
	- Forget bigger picture: think **broadly (what to do 5-10 years)**; why is MSRP important to reaching your goal
- Present their work to **MIT faculties & staffs**
