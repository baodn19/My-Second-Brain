## 1st Pass
### Problems
- *Existing detection methodologies:* dense clusters, occlusions, overlaps, and lighting conditions
- *Technical:* low efficiency, high computational expenses, and subpar accuracy involving small and densely packed targets
### Contributions
- *Limitation:* confined to image datasets $\implies$ Real-world testing
- *YOLOv11-GSF:* combine [[YOLOv11]] and [[Ghost Convolution]] method
- *C3K2SG:* combine [[self-moving point convolution]] and [[convolutional gated linear units]]
- *F-PloUv2:* inspired by [[Focaler IoU]] ad [[PloUv2]]
- *Statistics:* average precision of 97.8%, an accuracy of 95.99%, and a recall rate of 93.62%, representing improvements of 1.8%, 1.3 percentage points, and 2.1% over the original YOLOv11.
