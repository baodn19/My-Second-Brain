- **Balanced Distribution:** You have a dataset of 1,000 images for a binary classifier. 500 images are labeled "Cat" and 500 are labeled "Dog" (a **50/50 split**).
- **Imbalanced Distribution:** You have 1,000 images. 990 are "Background" and only 10 are "Traffic Cone" (a **99/1 split**). This is extremely common in computer vision and robotics.
