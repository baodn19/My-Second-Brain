# Concept

- The workmen in simple operation who naturally find an easier way to execute his job

He wants to have more time to play with his friend so he attached a string from the handle of the valve that controls the communication to another part of the machine which does the work for him.
- Machine made by expert and inventors whose job is always influenced by the [[division of labor]]. This help save times and improve dexterity of the makers.
