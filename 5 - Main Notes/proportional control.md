# Overview
- *Definition:* Signal becomes proportional to the error, the difference between the measured and the setpoint.
- *Effect:* decreases response time (quickly gets to the [[desired state]]) but increases overshoot.
$$
K_p \cdot e(t)
$$
# Variables
- $K_{p}$: proportional coefficient
- e(t): [[feedback error]]
