# Key Takeaways
- *Core principle:* write in your own word all the time, everything is knowledge, irreducible & complete notes, knowledge is interconnected 
- *[[Fleeting]] notes:* capture ideas
- *Reference notes:* note where you found it, quote, little thoughts
- *[[Evergreen]] notes:* irreducible, your own word, paragraph form; go through reference and fleeting notes to include important bits
