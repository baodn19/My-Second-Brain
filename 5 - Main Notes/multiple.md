- *Definition:* $a\in \mathbb{Z}:a\mathbb{Z}=\{a\cdot k|k\in \mathbb{Z}\}=\{\dots,-2a,-a,0,a,2a,\dots\}$
- *Prove:* If a|b $\implies b\mathbb{Z}\subseteq a\mathbb{Z}$
		Assume a|b $\implies \exists m\in \mathbb{Z}:b=am$
		Take $x \in b\mathbb{Z}\implies \exists l\in \mathbb{Z}:x=bl$
		Since $b=am\implies x=aml=a(ml)$
		Since $ml\in \mathbb{Z}\implies$x is an integer multiple of a
		$\implies x \in a\mathbb{Z}$  $\Box$
