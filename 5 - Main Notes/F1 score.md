# Overview
- *Definition:* the [[harmonic mean]] that represents the balance between **Precision** and **Recall** (refer to [[precision-recall (PR) curve]]).
$$
F_{1}=2 \cdot \frac{Precision \times Recall}{Precision + Recall}
$$
- High $F_{1}$ score means high precision and high recall
