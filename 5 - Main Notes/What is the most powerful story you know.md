## Obvious Answer
- *How did the story shape me:* how privilege my desire to improve other people's lives
- Writer, teacher [[Nguyễn Ngọc Kí]]:
	- Born in Nam Dinh, Northern Vietnam 1947
	- Both hands paralyzed at age of 4
	- at 7: he stands outside of classes and come home with a small brick between his two toes
	- He starts with letters like O, V -> Draw shapes with rulers and compass 
	- at 16: place 5th at Math Olympiad in the Northern region
	- After graduating, he went back and became a teacher in his hometown
	- He brought value to generations by inspiring them and showing them that if you put your mind, your feet, (and arms if you have them) you can do anything
- I want to bring values to other people, my community, through my work in automation
- I realized how lucky I am to have a healthy body and healthy mind => Take it as my responsibility to bring values to other
