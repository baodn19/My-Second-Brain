## Post-graduation slip
- [[What's your opinion on pirating]]
	- *Feedback:* must research thoroughly before expressing opinion 
				show what you think -> how you think about it: framework, theory
- Reviewer wants to see big ambition => Start **"I aspire ..."** (a [[manifesto]])
	- *Answer:* why the program -> how does your work tie to the UK -> General plan post-scholarship
- Make [[list of interested UK faculty]]
	- Correspond for a substantial time, work with other faculty to create a proposal
	- Support the answer of *Why UK?*
- Requirements:
	- *Necessary:* technically proficient
	- *Sufficient:* a people person, aspiration to make a change to the world, 
				openness to learning => Be able to hold my ground on an opinion I don't agree -> [[How is knowledge today constructed]]
- *Interview & Weekly meeting:* to confirm they picked the right person
	- *Tips:* study the purpose of the program;
			[[How are you going to use the resource of the Gates Scholarship]]
			[[How are you going to use the resource of the Knight-Hennessy Scholarship]]

## Interview
- *Asking for clarification:*
	- 1st question: DO NOT
	- Last question & you've good impressions: maybe
- *[[graceful pivot]]*
- [[What would winning the Gates today mean for you]]
- [[What would winning the Knight-Hennessy today mean for you]]
- Moral idea must end on confident notes => Support aspiration to **help people**
### Explain to your parents
- Begin with a story related to the listener
- **DO NOT** start with "I don't have to explain ..."

### Most powerful story
- *Takeaway:* what does it say about you
	- Is the answer consistent with your documents
	- Bring **YOU** into your field and limit technical jargons
- **DO NOT** repeat the prompt

### Why pick me
- **DO NOT** get too detailed too quickly
- Start catchy, flashy (begins with conclusion) -> Ease into the points
	- Start with most important -> Embellish later
	- **DO NOT** be too much on the nose
