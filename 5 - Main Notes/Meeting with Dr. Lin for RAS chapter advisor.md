## Note
- *RAS @ USF:*
	- *Problem:* a lack of professional development in the field of robotics & automation & no resources related to research
	- *Mission:* 
		- Technical: entry-level workshop on ROS2, Gazebo, RViz, OpenCV, ... (abundant online material)
		- Research: familiarize students with robotics research at USF, prepare for summer REUs
		- Industry: invite professional for seminar and connect students with experts
	- *Vision:* nurturing robotics community @ USF & educate the interdisciplinary nature of robotics through collaboration with other organizations
- *Requirement:*
	- An active IEEE member of Graduate Student Member grade or higher
	- Faculty Member of the school
	- An active member of RAS
- *Annual fee:* ~$229 + $9 = ~$238 (50% off if register from March 1st)
- *Duties:*
	- Promote IEEE as a progressive technical information provider to local industry and its employees and the role IEEE plays in developing new technologies.
	- Create a small committee to assist you in planning and carrying out the work associated with this position; ensure your committee members understand their roles and expectations.
	- Engage and involve Section volunteers (Section Chair, Section Student Activities Chair and Section Student Representative) to support the Branch activities.
	- Ask your colleagues to partner with you to offer technical demonstrations, professional development workshops, and visits to industry for your Branch Members.
	- Support and encourage student volunteers who are involved with programs and activities and recognize volunteers who contribute to the Branch’s success.
