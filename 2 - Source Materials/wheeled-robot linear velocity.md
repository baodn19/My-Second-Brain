# Overview
- *Definition:* the linear velocity of a [[differential robot]]
$$
v = \frac{v_{l}+v_{r}}{2} =\omega R
$$
# Variables
- $v_{l}$: [[left wheel linear velocity]]
- $v_{r}$: [[right wheel linear velocity]]
- $\omega$: [[wheeled-robot angular velocity]]
- R: [[distance to rotation center at ICC]]
