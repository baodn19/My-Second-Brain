- *Definition:* $A \times B=\{(x,y)|x \in A, y \in B\}$ where (x, y) is an [[ordered pair]]
			$A_{1}\times A_{2}\times\dots \times A_{k}=\{(a_{1},\dots,a_{k})|a_{i} \in A_{i} 1 \leq i\leq k$
- *Properties:* $A \times B \neq B\times A$
- *Example:* $\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}=\mathbb{R}^2$:  xy-plane
