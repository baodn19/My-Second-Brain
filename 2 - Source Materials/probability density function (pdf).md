- *Definition:* the probability of an interval; applies to **continuous random variable**
$$
p(a\leq X\leq b)=\int_{a}^{b}f(x)  \, dx
$$
- *Properties:* 
	- Always non-negative
	- Total area under the curve equals 1
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)  \, dx=1
$$
