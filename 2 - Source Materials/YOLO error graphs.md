- *What to look for:* downward trends
- *box_loss:* How "wrong" the bounding box ([[intersection over union (IoU)]])coordinates are
- *cls_loss:* "Classification Loss." Is it confusing a strawberry with a leaf?
- *dfl_loss:* How wide is the range for the specific location of each bounding box edges
