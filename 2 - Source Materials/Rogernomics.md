- *Definition:* extensive free-market economic reforms implemented in New Zealand starting in 1984 by Finance Minister [[Roger Douglas]]. It represents New Zealand's rapid shift from **a highly regulated, protectionist economy** to **a deregulated, open market.**
- *Similarities:* [[Reaganomics]] (US), [[Thatcherism]] (UK)
