- *Source:* [Vocal Branding: How Your Voice Shapes Your Communication Image | Wendy LeBorgne | TEDxUCincinnati](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p_ylzGfHKOs)
- *Intensity (Loudness):* middle ground, not loud and not quiet; project more in larger room
- *Inflection (Intonation):* Go up to emphasize and end a statement with down **(especially when defending an idea)**
	- Upward: signal excitement about future applications or open research questions
	- Downward: state past results and data
- *Rate (Speed):* average 140-150 wpm, change speed to separate idea and emphasize idea
	- Slow down: introducing novel technical concepts or critical metrics.
	- Pause: between distinct ideas
- *Frequency:* low voice is better
- *Quality:* clarity
