- *Definition:* $d(x, O_{1})+d(O_{1},O_{2})+\dots+d(O_{n}, q_{goal})$
	- x: current position
	- $O_{n}$: the endpoints that define [[interval of continuity]] that intersects the segment between x and $q_{goal}$
	- d: distance
	- $q_{goal}$: the goal position
