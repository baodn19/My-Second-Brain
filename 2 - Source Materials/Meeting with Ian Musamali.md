# Prep Information
## Funding
- *RAS:* [Support for Chapters - IEEE Robotics and Automation Society Website](https://www.ieee-ras.org/ras-chapters/support-for-chapters/)
	- **Start-up Grant:** $500 USD
	- **Initiative Grant:** $2,000 USD annually (competitive basis)
	- **Distinguished Lecturer (DL) Travel Support:** up to **$3,000 USD** to help defray the travel expenses of a Society-appointed Distinguished Lecturer to visit and speak at a local chapter.
- *IEEE Region 3:*
	- **Professional Activities Committee (PAC) Funding:** up to **$1,000 USD** per event. ([Professional Activities - IEEE Region 3](https://r3.ieee.org/committees/professional-activities/))
	- **Young Professionals (YP) Event Funding:** [Event Funding - Young Professionals](https://yp.ieee.org/volunteers/event-funding/)
	    - **Local Activity Funding:** Maximum award of **$750 USD**.
        - **Meet-up Funding:** Maximum award of **$1,500 USD**.
	     - **STEP (Student Transition and Elevation Partnership):** Maximum award of **$500 USD** to support students transitioning to professional membership.
- *Florida West Coast Section (FWCS):*
	- **Student Branch Support:** between **$200 and $500 USD** for student branch events upon request and approval by the Section Executive Committee (ExCom).
	- **Travel Subsidies:** The Section often provides subsidies for students to attend **SoutheastCon**
