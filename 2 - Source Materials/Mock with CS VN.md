## Feedback
- *Self-intro:*
	- Too broad, too much technical for a interview
	- Work well in the team: 1-2 sentence to shape personality & working style
	- Tailor to the role specifically
	- Work in projects similar to the company: high-level technical skills
	- Year and school -> Experience at ... -> Skills (1-2 sentence) -> Projects: ... -> Hobby
- *Why CS:* depends
	- Enthusiastic, projects that bring value to family & connects
	- Make it easy to empathize
- *Experience:*
	- No depth: discuss more about the thought process and procedure of the project
	- What didn't work well/ work well: expected more behavioral; how I adapt to new concepts, learn on the go
	- Conflict: should use STAR and CARL
	- See pros and cons in options
	- 70% Behavioral + 30% Technical
	- How to ask questions & How to learn
- *Soft skills:* elaborate things better, depth of the action, have an idea of what personality to show
	- Why company: look for internal info, company specific, conversational based
	- Split up: technical + innovation + both sided conversations
	- Read the room
- *Prepare questions:* at least 2 questions
	- Follow up on their response
	- Remember their team and talk about how long they stay in the company
- *TLDR:* tailor to STAR, and bullet point
