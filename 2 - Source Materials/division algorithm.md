- *Definition:* if $n,d\in \mathbb{Z}\implies \exists!q,r\in \mathbb{Z}:$
	- q: quotient
	- r: remainder
$$
n =dq+r;0\leq r<|d|
$$
- *Notation:* divide n by d & n = dq + r: n $\equiv$ r(mod d)
	- [[divisor]]: $m \equiv n$ (mod d) $\iff m =dk+n$ for some $k\in \mathbb{Z} \iff d|(m-n)$
