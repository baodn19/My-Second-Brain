- *Robot's state:* The circle of radius R initially becomes tangent to the obstacle and then slits the obstacle an [[interval of continuity]] defined by two endpoints $O_{i}$ ($O_{1}$ and $O_{2}$).
- *Function:* the robot then moves towards $O_{i}$ that minimizes the [[Heuristic Distance to Goal]].
