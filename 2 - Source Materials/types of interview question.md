# Essential
- Regardless of the types, you must balance between theory and individual by giving snippets and examples of things I've done.

# Definitional Q.
- Can be take from any course on the transcripts or item on the resume
- Bring up classic works and [[Quotes]]
	- Make sure the context of the quotes aligns with the answer
	- *Eg:* don't use [[Everything should be as simple as it can be, but not simpler - Albert Einstein]] for complexity in economics

# Counterfactual/ Hypothetical Q.
- *Def:* twist the world and see what would it change from you
- Define the parameters before answering ([[graceful pivot]])

# Aspirational/ Experiential Q.
- *Def:* leadership activities; what do these experience teach you?
- Wouldn't work without ambition
