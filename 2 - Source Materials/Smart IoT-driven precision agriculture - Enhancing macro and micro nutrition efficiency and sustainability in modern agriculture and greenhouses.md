# 1st Pass: Extract information into headings: research question, problems solved, contributions, and limitations
## Research question
- How can [[IoT]] sensing be translated into sensor-cited, language-appropriate recommendations that smallholders trust and act on?
## Problems
- The adoption gap between modern IoT sensing potential and actual use by smallholder farmers in low- and middle-income countries.
- The difficulty of converting raw sensor data into understandable, actionable guidance for farmers lacking technical training.
- Low user engagement with conventional IoT dashboards caused by low literacy, limited internet access, and unfamiliar user interfaces.
## Contributions
- Developed the first end-to-end framework linking live sensor streams with a large language model to deliver localized agricultural advice over WhatsApp.
- Designed a modular software pipeline (intent, retrieval, synthesis, proactive alerts) that ensures evidence grounding, traceability, and multilingual support for low-literacy users.
- Provided empirical evidence from a 90-day deployment showing that farmers engaged daily with the chatbot and made concrete decisions, such as delaying irrigation or correcting soil acidity.

## Limitations
- The system lacked awareness of crop lifecycles, reducing its contextual relevance across different growth stages.
- The brief 15-day evaluation period for the chatbot phase restricted observation of long-term utility.
- System faithfulness dropped on complex queries that required synthesizing data from multiple sources like sensors, weather forecasts, and agronomic text.
- The pilot study was structured to assess usability and system robustness rather than statistically significant crop yield improvements.
