## 1st Pass
### Problems
- [[Strawberries are occluded by backgrounds]]
- [[Vision models are computationally expensive and parameter-heavy]]

### Contributions
- [[Pela block reduces parameter and background interference]]
- [[LAFPN enhance multi-scale detection]]
- [[Focaler-PIoU loss function direct model toward moderate quality anchor box]]
- [[Complex dataset from many sources]]
	- [[Create a dataset for winter strawberry]]
- 90.1 FPS on [[Jetson AGX Orin]] with [[FP16 quantization]]
### Limitation
- [[Three-stage classification of ripeness simplifies strawberries' maturation]]
- [[Generalized dataset is limited under extreme condition]]
