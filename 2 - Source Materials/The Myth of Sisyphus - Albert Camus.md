# Preface
- *Thesis:* whether life has a meaning and why do people suicide
	> The fundamental subject of "The Myth of Sisyphus" is this: it is legitimate and necessary to wonder whether life has a meaning; therefore it is legitimate to meet the problem of suicide face to face. (Page ix)
- *Purpose:* to live and create despite the **absurdity**
	> Although "The Myth of Sisyphus" poses mortal problems, it sums itself up for me as a lucid invitation to live and to create, in the very midst of the desert. (Page ix)
