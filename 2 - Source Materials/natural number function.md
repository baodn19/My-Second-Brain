- *Definition:* $\rho: \mathbb{N}\times \mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{Z},\rho(m,n)=m-n$
- *Prove:* $\rho: \mathbb{N}\times \mathbb{N}\to \mathbb{Z}, \rho((m,n))=m-n$ isn't a [[one-to-one (injective) function]]
		$\rho((0,0))=0-0=0$ and $\rho((1,1))=1-1=0$  $\textreferencemark$
- *Prove:* $\rho$ is an [[onto (surjective) function]]
		Take $m \in \mathbb{Z}:$ 
		If $m\geq0,\rho((m,0))=m-0=m$
		If $m <0,\rho((0,-m))=0-(-m)=m$
