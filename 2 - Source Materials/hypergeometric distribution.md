- *Application (discrete random variable):* Models the number of successes ($x$) in a sample ($n$) drawn without replacement from a finite population ($N$) containing $M$ successes.
- *Example:* Drawing a sample of 20 microcontrollers from a limited batch of 500 (where 30 are known to be faulty) to determine the probability of drawing exactly 2 faulty units.
