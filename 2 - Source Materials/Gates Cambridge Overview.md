- [[Gates Cambridge Eligibility]]: pursue a postgraduate degree in any subject available at the University of Cambridge
	- PhD in Engineering or PhD in Computer Science
- *Criteria:*
	-  outstanding intellectual ability
	- reasons for choice of course
	- a commitment to improving the lives of others
	- leadership potential
- *Mission:* build a global network of future leaders committed to improving the lives of others
	- Method: electing outstanding scholars, providing them with financial and non-financial support at one of the world’s leading universities and facilitating community building at and beyond Cambridge
