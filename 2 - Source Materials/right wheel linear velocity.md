# Overview
- *Definition:* linear velocity of right wheel
$$
v_l = r\varphi_{r} = \omega \left( R + \frac{d}{2} \right)
$$
# Variables
- $v_{r}$: right wheel linear velocity
- $\omega$: [[wheeled-robot angular velocity]]
- $d$: distance between wheels (axis length)
- r: radius of wheel
- $\varphi_{r}$: right [[wheel angular velocity]]
