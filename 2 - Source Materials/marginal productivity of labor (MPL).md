- *Definition:* additional output created by adding one more unit of labor (e.g., one worker or one hour of work), while keeping all other production factors—such as capital and land—constant.
# Formula
- *Discrete:*
	- $\bigtriangleup Q:$ Change in total output (quantity)
	- $\bigtriangleup L:$ Change in units of labor (typically 1)
$$
MPL=\frac{\bigtriangleup Q}{\bigtriangleup L}
$$
- *Continuous:*
$$
MPL=\frac{\partial Q}{\partial L}
$$
