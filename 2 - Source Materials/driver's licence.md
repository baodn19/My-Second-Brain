# Legal Documents
- [ ] Passport with visa
- [ ] I-20
- [ ] I-94
- [ ] SSN (if not, Letter of Ineligibility from Social Security Administration)
- [ ] Residential address: A residential lease or rental agreement, a bank statement dated within the last 60 days.

# Procedure
1. Complete the TLSAE Course (4hr, online)
2. Schedule an Appointment through the **Hillsborough County Tax Collector’s** website
3. Pass exams
- **Vision & Hearing Test:** Conducted at the counter during your appointment.
- **Class E Knowledge Exam:** 50 multiple-choice questions on road signs and laws.
- **Driving Skills Test:** You must provide your own vehicle with proof of valid Florida insurance and registration.
- In Florida, a "Safe Driver" discount is often awarded after maintaining a clean record (no tickets or at-fault accidents) for **three to five years**.
