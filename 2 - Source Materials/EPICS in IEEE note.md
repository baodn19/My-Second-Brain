# Notice while going through learning course
- Ask your institutional review board (IRB) before asking members of the public to engage with your project. Project proposals that have already undergone an IRB or ethical review process, please include that documentation in the application. Unsure if this applies to your project? Reach out to our committee epicsinieee@ieee.org.
## Engineering Design
- *Main Question:* Are students significantly engaged in the engineering design process (from problem identification through testing and re-design)?
- **“Keep It Simple and Straightforward”** $\implies$ Easier to be maintained by the community $\implies$ Answer: **Who's maintaining the project afterwards?**
- Adding technology should be done for strategic purposes
## Level of Innovation
- Completing appropriate background research about existing solutions
- *Main Questions:*
	- Are there any examples of solutions similar to your project?
	- Is your project an improvement of an existing idea?
	- Does your project involve the modification of a technology?

## Non-Profit Alignment
- *Main Questions:* Does the project align well with the needs of the non-profit?
	- Who are the potential end users?
	- Which organization has a mission that matches?
	- Does the organization need technological support?
	- Which local community partners are close to the physical site?
- Ask for a collaboration letter for the development, deployment, and maintenance of the project
- Provide a **clear maintenance plan**

## Deployment & Project Sustainability
- *Steps:*
	1. Define: Deliverables, When?, Where?, Team involved, and Benefits
	2. Plan: Resources, Risks, Roles, Dependencies, and Timeline
	3. Test: Functionality, Performance, Compatibility, and Security
	4. Deploy: Install, Configure, Data transfer, Backup, and Verify
	5. Evaluate: User feedback, Efficiency, Reliability, Usability, and Effectiveness
- *Sustainable projects:* Adaptability, Ease of implementation, Scalability, and Maintainability
- *Main questions:* What group or organization will continue or sustain project implementations after the project is complete?

## Student Learning Outcomes
- *Framework:*  
	- Skill: Bloom’s taxonomy (Vanderbilt University Center for Teaching)
	- Outreach:  SMART strategy
- As much as possible, try to find an academic motivator (class project, elective credits, capstone project, etc.) $\implies$ Reach out out to seniors so this project can be capstone
- Rather see a handful of technical and nontechnical skills that are identified strategically based on the specifics of the project.

## Project Budget
- Make sure to save your receipts!

## Impact Assessment
- Prefers that you report direct impact (those individuals who are directly engaging with the solution to receive a positive benefit)
- Track how many people will realistically use the solution in the specified time frame
