# Concept
- Strawberry provides great nutritional value and is used in many recipes [1]. It is hailed as the "Queen of Fruits" in Western and East Asian culture. 
- Strawberry has high economic value, especially "winter strawberries" grown in Florida: [[Florida is the dominant winter strawberry supplier in US]] 
- In the US - 2023, strawberries were the third-most-produced fruit [2].  
- *Drawback:* [[Strawberry requires timely harvesting]]
