# Evergreen note
- *Characteristic:* 
	- atomic
	- densely linked
	- written in your own word
	- requires many revision and development
- *Life cycle:*
	1. Seed: First captured the idea, unstructured, unlinked
	2. Incubating: translating the idea into your own words, summarize to core premise, establishing initial links
	3. Evergreen: refer *Characteristic*

# Literature note
## Collect
- *Highlighting:*
	- Date & Important events
	- Interesting quotes
- Encounter ideas that relates to interest: record in notebook with special interest & page number

## Process
- Compile dates together and takes note of connections between events
