- *Definition:* a large-scale artificial intelligence model trained on massive quantities of data (often web-scale) that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks.
- *Key characteristics:*
	- **Scale and Pretraining:** They are initially pre-trained on broad data to acquire general knowledge, semantics, and representations before being specialized.
	- **Adaptability:** They are designed to act as a base that can be fine-tuned or prompted to perform various specific tasks, rather than being built from scratch for a single, narrow application.
	- **Reasoning Capability:** In contexts like physical AI, they are often used as reasoners to handle high-level task planning, generating instructions or visual traces that guide specialized execution.
