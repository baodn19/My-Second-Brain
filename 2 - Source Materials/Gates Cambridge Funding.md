# Core Components
- Tuition
- maintenance allowance (**£21,000** for 12 months at the 2024-25 rate)
	- Get reduce or remove if have salary or another substantial scholarship
- one economy single airfare at both the beginning and end of the course
- inbound visa costs & the cost of the Immigration Health Surcharge

# Discretionary Components
- *Academic development funding:* dependent on course length, for attend conferences & courses $\implies$ **£500 - £2,000**
- *Hardship funding:* for unforeseen difficulties
- *Not covered:* bench fees, scientific equipment, or academic resources
