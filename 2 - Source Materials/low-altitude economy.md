- *Definition:* flight activities conducted within the low-altitude domain — typically between 1,000 and 3,000 meters — using manned or unmanned aerial vehicles like drones.
- China is leading the world in LAE:
	- The first nation to formally integrate LAE provisions into its civil aviation laws in 2025
	- The Greater Bay Area (GBA) has formed two important “low-altitude economic cross-belts”: a manufacturing belt centered on Guangzhou, Zhuhai, and Foshan, and a financial services belt centered on Hong Kong, Macao, and Shenzhen
	- Pioneers: **DJI, EHang, and AutoFlight** 
- *Challenge:*
	- Indirect emissions from using electricity made by fossil fuels
	- Noise & privacy
	- Price
