- *Definition:* Events A and B are mutually exclusive if either event A or event B, but not jointly, can occur on a single performance of an experiment
- *[[joint probability]]*: $P(A\cap B)=0$
- *The [[probability]] of events A or B occurring*: $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)$
