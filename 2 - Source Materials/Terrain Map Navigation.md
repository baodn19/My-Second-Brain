# Research Question
- *Main question:* Are SOTA VLMs capable of comprehending terrain map?
- *Sub questions:*
	- How can Vision Language Models (VLMs) be utilized to extract geometric attributes, classify landforms, and generate natural language navigation routes (both on-road and off-trail) using 3D topographic maps and Digital Elevation Models (DEMs)?
	- How can Map Space Scene Graphs (MSSG) and Terrain Scene Graphs (TSG) be improved to reduce VLM recognition errors regarding elevations and landmarks?
	- Does representing elevation with different colors yield better VLM recognition performance than traditional contour lines?
	- How does a "Global first, Local second" methodology—where the model analyzes a global map and then zooms into local waypoints—improve the VLM's navigation accuracy?

# Search Strategy
- *Smash:*
	- Using VLM for 3D map
	- Different form of terrain maps
