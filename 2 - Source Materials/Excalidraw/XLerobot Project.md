# Reflection


## What I learned
- Communicating technical concepts to the general public: learn to choose the right analogy to explain paper's methodology
	- For people new to imitation learning, I like to use the analogy of teach the robot like teach a toddler. You'll have to guide its hand (teleoperation), and it will learn from those demonstration based on its existing knowledge (fine-tuning a VLA policy) and execute the task by itself
- **Technical**: become more familiar with imitation learning policy like ACT or VLA policy like pi0.5
- Gained an appreciation for the hard labor that is data collection and teleoperation, especially for VR teleoperation. Sometimes, I'm 9/10 recorded episodes in, the VR headset lose connection, and there goes 15 minutes of my life. 
	- Results: 50 episodes of picking K-cup and dropping into a box and the robot runs 4/5 times. 
- All is fine since I'm good enough to teach people how to teleoperate and see the awe in their face.
