# Interview
## Overview
- *Types of interview:*
	- Academic: by nerds for nerds, little room for imagination
	- Expansive: not enough to answer the questions directly/ in an obvious manner
- *Interviewer:*
	- Interviewer try to understand the underlying philosophy of what you do
	- 1 primary interviewer closest to your fields
	- Will be known beforehand
	- They want to know what you are bringing to the tables and others don't => Non-obvious answers help
	- Utilize stories (histories) so other interviewers can understand
	- They don't want you to spend your whole life in academia
## Definitional Question
- Can be take from any course on the transcripts or item on the resume

## Counterfactual Question
- *Def:* twist the world and see what would it change from you
- Define the parameters before answering
## Experiential Question
- *Def:* leadership activities; what do these experience teach you?
- Wouldn't work without ambition
- 
## Behavioral
- How you say matter & put your hands under the table
- Speak at a certain rate, know how many words you can fit in 10 seconds
- Answer with ethics in mind
- Don't get hung up if the answer isn't correct
- Don't try to read the room
- *Graceful pivot:* admit that you don't know something and pivot to something related that you know
- *Never say -> Do say:*
	- "That's a good question!" -> "I've struggled with ... and here's where I stand on ..."
	- "I hope that answers your question." -> "I can elaborate if you want."
